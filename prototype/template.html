Show the formulas

Heat stress. WBGT = 0.7·natural wet bulb + 0.2·globe + 0.1·air, each solved from temperature, humidity, sun angle and wind (Liljegren 2008).

Heat bands. Not heat-adapted: 18 / 23 / 28 °C (ACSM). Heat-adapted: +4.4 °C, the ISO 7243 acclimatisation offset at running effort, so indoors above 32.4 °C (US Army Category 5 starts at 32.2 °C).

Heat budget (simplified ISO 7933). Heat made M = MET·1.163·kg. Shed by air and radiation A·(h_c(T_skin − T_air) + 0.72·h_r(T_skin − T_radiant)), by breathing, and by sweat up to E_max = A·16.5·h_c·(p_skin − p_air), capped by maximum sweat (400 W/m², +25 % heat-adapted). Heat that cannot be shed is stored: core rises by S/(kg·3490) per second on top of the steady core 36.6 + 0.0036·M/A. The plan keeps the core below 39 °C.

Cardiovascular load. RR = exp(H + P). H = 0.00508·max(0, WBGT − local reference). P = max over pollutants of β·f·max(0, C − WHO), β from Stafoggia 2023 at the local temperature percentile, f = (MET − 1.55)·minutes / (1.55·1440).

Air guard rails. PM2.5 hourly above 60 / 120 / 250 µg/m³ (India NAQI bands), ozone above 180 / 240 (EU thresholds). Cold. Wind chill at your running airflow: −10 / −27 / −40 °C. Pollen. 50 grains/m³ with hay fever on.

Prototype for demonstration, not medical advice. Weather: Open-Meteo and ERA5; air: CAMS (Europe for Amsterdam, global for Chennai). Opened as a file on a computer, the page fetches today's forecast for the chosen city; inside the published link it uses today's readings captured on 13 Nov. Chennai's global air grid is coarse and reads low for city streets.
